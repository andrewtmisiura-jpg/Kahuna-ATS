# Applicant Tracking System (ATS): Build Brief

## Context
I own Kahuna Window Cleaning and Midwest Access (Twin Cities, MN). We're building our own applicant tracking system to replace paper applications and spreadsheets. It needs to work for two roles right away:

- **Snow Removal Technician (on-call):** hiring now, fall 2026. Light filtering, we intentionally over-hire. This is the test run.
- **Window Cleaning Technician (full-time seasonal):** hiring spring 2027. Heavy filtering. This is the main use case.

Build it so new roles can be added later without code changes.

Start by reading this brief, asking me any questions that block the design, then proposing a plan and data model before writing code. Build in phases (see bottom). Phase 1 needs to be live in about two to three weeks.

## Our Hiring Process (what the software supports)
1. **Indeed** posting with screener (knockout) questions. Pass = invite to a group interview. This happens inside Indeed, not in our ATS. We may optionally log the Indeed source and apply date.
2. **Group interview.** In-person presentation. Attendees sign a paper sign-up sheet; staff quietly "star" the best fits with a one-line reason. At the end we show a QR code.
3. **QR application.** Scanning the QR code is when the applicant enters our ATS.
4. **Review.** AI scores the application against our criteria and makes a recommendation. A human makes the decision.
5. **One-on-one interview** (30 min + ladder test) → conditional offer → background check → hired.
6. **Retention tracking** after hire, so we can learn which application answers predict long-term fit.

Snow removal may skip the group interview and go straight to the application link. Pipelines and stages should be configurable per role.

## Recommended Stack (push back if you see something better)
- **Hosting/DB:** Railway with Postgres. Do not use Airtable.
- **App:** Next.js (TypeScript) with an ORM like Prisma or Drizzle.
- **Admin auth:** Google sign-in restricted to our Google Workspace domains (we're fully on Google).
- **SMS:** Twilio (or similar). Note A2P 10DLC registration can take a couple of weeks, so start that early and support an email magic link as a fallback (Resend or similar).
- **AI scoring:** Claude API.
- Mobile-first applicant pages (people fill it out on their phones). Admin works well on desktop and phone.

## Applicant Experience
### QR landing and save-and-resume
1. QR code opens a short landing page for that role and (optionally) that group interview session.
2. Applicant enters first name, last name, mobile number, email, and checks a consent box for texts.
3. We immediately text (and email) them a personal, unique link to their application.
4. Application **autosaves every field**. They can start in the room and finish at home. The link reopens their saved progress.
5. If not submitted after 24 hours, send one templated reminder text with the link.
6. Track status: Started vs Submitted, so we can see drop-off.

QR codes should be generated by the app per role and per group interview session so we know which session each applicant came from.

### Application questions
Questions must be configurable per role (admin editable) and **versioned**, so old answers stay tied to the exact question wording they answered. Question types: short text, long text, single choice, multi choice, yes/no, date.

Starter set for Window Cleaning:
- Work history: last two jobs, how long, why you left
- Earliest start date; confirm full-time availability for the season
- Valid driver's license (yes/no); reliable transportation to our Golden Valley shop (yes/no)
- Tell us about the hardest physical job you've done. What made it hard and how did you handle it?
- Why this job, and why now?
- Busy weeks here are 55 hours, slow weeks are much lighter. How would you handle a slow stretch?
- Tell us about a time you had to talk to a stranger and help them or convince them of something.
- What would your last supervisor say is your biggest strength, and the thing you most need to work on?
- Tell us about a time you were uncomfortable or nervous doing something and pushed through anyway.
- Where do you want to be in two years?
- How did you hear about us?

Starter set for Snow Removal: on-call overnight/early morning availability, license, transportation, equipment experience (snowblower, shovel, plow, skid steer, tractor), other job schedule, how quickly they can respond to a call.

**Do not include:** criminal history (Minnesota ban-the-box, that waits until after a conditional offer), date of birth or age, SSN, or anything about protected characteristics.

## Admin Experience
- **Pipeline board** per role (kanban by stage) plus a filterable list view.
- Default stages: Applied (started) → Submitted → Reviewed → One-on-One Invited → One-on-One Done → Conditional Offer → Background Check → Hired, plus Disqualified and Withdrew at any point (with a reason picklist).
- **Every stage change is logged with a timestamp and the user who made it.** This drives all the time-based reports.
- Applicant profile: answers, AI score and rationale, group interview star and note, staff notes, message history, stage history.
- **Group interview sessions:** create a session (date, location, role), generate its QR code, enter the sign-up sheet names, mark stars with a reason. Match sign-up entries to applicants who later apply.
- **Templated messages** (group interview invite, one-on-one invite with scheduling, reminder, decline). Staff trigger them; texts come from a real business number. **No AI-written or AI-conversational messages to applicants.** All applicant-facing messaging is templated and human-triggered (or a simple scheduled reminder).

## AI Scoring
- Each role has a **rubric** (admin-editable, versioned): a list of criteria, what a strong / acceptable / weak answer looks like, and weights.
- On submit, send the application answers to the Claude API with the rubric. **Do not send name, phone, email, address, or anything not job-related**, to keep scoring focused and reduce bias.
- Return structured JSON: score per criterion, overall score (0 to 100), recommendation (Advance / Review / Decline), short rationale, and any red flags (e.g. mentions another job conflicting with full-time hours).
- Store the score with the rubric version and model used. Allow re-scoring.
- **The AI never moves an applicant or rejects anyone.** A human records the decision. Store both the AI recommendation and the human decision so we can later see how often they agree.

## Hires and Retention
- A Hires page: hire date, role, crew, and status (Active / Left).
- When someone leaves: end date, voluntary or involuntary, reason picklist (part-time conflict, afraid of heights, too physical, seasonal hours, performance, attendance, no-show, other) plus notes, and rehire eligible (yes/no).
- Optional manager rating at 30 and 90 days.
- Auto-calculate retained at 30 / 60 / 90 days and end of season.

## Reports
- **Time to hire:** average and median days from submitted to offer, and from submitted to disqualified. Filter by role, date range, source.
- **Time in each stage**, so we can see bottlenecks.
- **Funnel conversion:** started → submitted → reviewed → interviewed → offered → hired → retained 90 days. By role, source, and group interview session.
- Group interview attendance vs applications from that session.
- **Retention** by role, source, and hire cohort.
- **Question predictiveness:** for each choice/yes-no question and for each AI criterion score, compare answers for people who stayed vs left. Show sample sizes clearly and warn when the sample is too small to mean anything (we'll only hire 10 to 20 window cleaners a season). Keep roles separate so snow data doesn't skew window cleaning analysis.
- AI recommendation vs human decision vs actual retention.
- CSV export of everything.

## Compliance and Data
- Consent checkbox for texts; honor STOP replies.
- Keep applicant records at least one year (federal record-keeping); make retention configurable. Background check results do **not** live in the ATS, only the status.
- Role-based access: Admin, Hiring Manager, Viewer.
- Audit log of stage changes and edits.

## Phases
**Phase 1 (target ~Nov 1, 2026, for snow hiring)**
Roles and configurable questions, QR landing with text/email resume link, autosaving application, admin pipeline board and list, stage history with timestamps, notes, Google sign-in, CSV export. Deploy on Railway.

**Phase 2**
AI scoring with rubrics, group interview sessions with QR per session and stars, templated SMS invites and reminders, disqualify/withdraw reasons.

**Phase 3 (before spring 2027)**
Hires and retention page, full reports, question-predictiveness analysis.

## Later (not now)
We're also building our own CRM. Keep the ATS as its own app with a clean API so it could connect to that later. Possible future: push hired employees into payroll/onboarding.
