# SMS Terms: Kahuna Window Cleaning Hiring

Effective: [OWNER INPUT: effective date]

These terms cover the text messages Kahuna Window Cleaning sends from **763-292-2259** as part of our hiring process. They go with our Privacy Policy at tidepool.kahunawindowcleaning.com/privacy.

## The program

- **Program name:** Kahuna Window Cleaning Hiring
- **Number we text from:** 763-292-2259
- **Who sends it:** Kahuna Window Cleaning ([OWNER INPUT: legal entity name]), Twin Cities, Minnesota

## Who gets these texts

We text only people who applied for a job with us on tidepool.kahunawindowcleaning.com and checked the box on the application form that says:

> Text me my application link and updates about this application (optional)

The box is unchecked until you check it. You can apply without checking it. If you leave it unchecked, we contact you by email instead.

## What we text about

If you checked the box, we may text you:

- Your personal link to finish your application.
- One reminder if you started but did not finish, usually the next day.
- Interview scheduling and interview reminders.
- Updates on the status of your application.
- Our decision on your application.

That is all. We do not text about sales, promotions or other job openings. Your consent covers this application only. If you apply again for a later opening, you will be asked again.

Texts that are not a reply to something you just did are sent between 9am and 8pm Central time.

## Message frequency

Message frequency varies. Most applicants receive only a few texts over the whole hiring process.

## Cost

Message and data rates may apply. That means your mobile carrier may charge you for the texts you receive or send under your plan. We do not charge you anything.

## How to stop: text STOP

Reply **STOP** to any text from us. You will get one confirmation text and then nothing more from us.

Please know that STOP applies to every Kahuna Window Cleaning number, not only the hiring line. Our texting provider blocks your number from all of our lines, including our customer line and, if you are hired, the number we use to text our crews. Our staff cannot undo this for you. If you want texts again, reply **START** to 763-292-2259.

You can also opt out by replying in your own words, for example "please stop texting me." A person reads every reply and will mark you opted out. STOP is faster because it works at once.

If you opt out, we use email instead for anything we need to tell you about your application.

## How to get help: text HELP

Reply **HELP** to any text from us and you will get a message with our contact details. You can also call or text 763-292-2259, or email [OWNER INPUT: hiring email].

## Carriers

Wireless carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages. We text most major US carriers. If our texts do not reach you, we will reach you by email.

## Your number

Give us a mobile number that is yours, or that you have permission to use. If you change or give up your number while your application is open, let us know so our texts do not go to someone else.

## Privacy

Our Privacy Policy at tidepool.kahunawindowcleaning.com/privacy explains what we collect and how we protect it. We do not share your mobile number or your text opt-in information with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.

## Changes to these terms

Each version of these terms is dated and kept. If we change them, we will post the new version here with a new effective date.
