# Text messages from Kahuna Window Cleaning Hiring

Kahuna Window Cleaning (Blue Skies Window Washing, LLC) is a window cleaning and snow removal company in the Twin Cities, Minnesota. When you apply for a job with us online, you can choose to get text messages about your application from our hiring number, **763-292-2259**. This page explains how that works.

## How you opt in

1. You open our job application at tidepool.kahunawindowcleaning.com. People reach it in one of these ways:
   - A link in a message from us on Indeed after you apply there.
   - A QR code we show at the end of a group interview.
   - A QR code or link on a flyer or sign.
   - A link on our website, [OWNER INPUT: website URL].
2. The first page shows the job, the pay and a short form: first name, last name, mobile number and email.
3. Under the mobile number field there is one checkbox. **It is unchecked until you check it, and you can apply without checking it.** The label reads:

   > Text me my application link and updates about this application (optional)

   Under the box, this paragraph explains what you are agreeing to:

   > By checking this box, you agree that Kahuna Window Cleaning may send you text messages at the mobile number above about your job application: your application link, one reminder if you don't finish, interview scheduling and reminders, status updates, and our decision. Message frequency varies. Msg & data rates may apply. Reply HELP for help or STOP to opt out at any time. Checking this box is not a condition of applying; if you leave it unchecked, we will email you instead. See our SMS Terms and Privacy Policy.

4. If you check the box and continue, your first text arrives within a minute or so. It carries your personal application link.
5. If you leave the box unchecked, nothing changes about your application. We email you the link instead, and every later message comes by email.

[Screenshot of the landing form goes here, showing the mobile field, the unchecked box, the paragraph under it and the two links.]

We keep a record of each person's choice: the box's state, the exact wording shown, the time, and the internet address of the device. We never pre-check the box, and we never text anyone who did not check it.

## What we text about

Only things about your application:

- Your personal application link, so you can finish on any device.
- One reminder if you started and did not finish, usually the next day.
- Interview scheduling and reminders.
- Updates on where your application stands.
- Our decision.

We do not send marketing, promotions or notices about other job openings. Your consent covers this one application. Message frequency varies; most applicants get only a few texts in total. Message and data rates may apply.

**Reply to HELP:**

## How to stop

Reply **STOP** to any text from us. You will get one confirmation and then no more texts. STOP blocks texts from every Kahuna Window Cleaning number, not only the hiring line, and our staff cannot undo it. To get texts again, reply **START** to 763-292-2259. If you opt out, we reach you by email instead.

You can also tell us in your own words, by text, email or phone, and a person will mark you opted out.

## How to get help

Reply **HELP** to any text, call or text 763-292-2259, or email [OWNER INPUT: hiring email].
